Biography & Research Statement

I am an AI Security Researcher specializing in privacy-preserving distributed systems, non-IID robust federated learning, and network intrusion detection. My research journey began with DDoS attack detection using ensemble machine learning during my undergraduate thesis at Prime University.

Currently, my work focuses on designing novel aggregation algorithms for heterogeneous client environments across UAV, IoT, and maritime radar systems. I have authored two first-author research papers published in Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio, Q1). My broader research interests encompass federated unlearning, feature heterogeneity, explainable AI (XAI) for cyber defense, and agentic AI systems.

Federated Learning & Non-IID Aggregation
Designing robust, privacy-preserving dynamic weighting algorithms under severe client data heterogeneity.
Autonomous Edge & Maritime Intrusion Detection
Developing real-time benchmarking frameworks for UAV swarm security and maritime radar intrusion defense.
Explainable AI & Cyber Defense
Leveraging ensemble ML, RFE feature selection, and side-channel traffic analysis for smart IoT resilience.
