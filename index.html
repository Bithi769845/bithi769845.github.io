Profile Summary

My research journey began with DDoS detection using ensemble machine learning during my undergraduate thesis, inspired by a professor's work in cybersecurity that first captivated me. This led me to specialize in Federated Learning for privacy-preserving security systems, where I have designed non-IID-robust aggregation algorithms for intrusion detection across UAV, IoT, and maritime radar systems, publishing two first-author papers in Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio, Q1). My research has since expanded into emerging directions within Federated Learning, including federated unlearning, feature heterogeneity, and healthcare applications, reflecting my broader interest in adapting Federated Learning and Cybersecurity to diverse real-world domains.
